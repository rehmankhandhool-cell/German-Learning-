# 🇩🇪 German Teacher

**Learn German. Live Confidently in Germany.**

German Teacher is a practical German-learning web application designed for international students, immigrants, workers, and newcomers living in or preparing to move to Germany.

The platform focuses on real-life German rather than only textbook grammar, helping learners communicate in everyday situations such as Anmeldung, doctors, university, work, shopping, transportation, restaurants, and more.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://germanteacher.store/

---

## ✨ Features

A structured beginner-friendly A1 course with 20 lessons covering:

- Greetings & Introductions
- German Alphabet & Pronunciation
- Numbers & Age
- Countries & Nationalities
- Family
- Daily Routine
- Time & Dates
- Food & Drinks
- Shopping
- Restaurant
- Home & Apartment
- Anmeldung & Address
- Doctor & Health
- Bus, Train & Transportation
- University
- Work & Jobs
- Phone Calls
- Bank
- Directions

Each lesson includes German examples, English translations, useful vocabulary, dialogues, grammar explanations, practice questions, and pronunciation audio.
